The sound is no better. Half the dialogue sounds like it was recorded in a cave, and some lines jump from a whisper to a shout for no reason. The footsteps must have been added by a guy stomping around in clown shoes, because they never match what's on screen.

The editing is worse. The camera sits on boring stuff for ages, then cuts to a doorknob. A doorknob! Props come and go between shots, and it turns from day to night in the middle of one conversation.

I'm not sure there was a script. If there was, it was a first draft scribbled on a napkin: clunky explanations, jokes that just die. Even the extras look lost, like they're wondering why they showed up. I know the feeling.

Watching paint dry would be more fun. At least the color changes. Honestly, someone should stop handing out indie film grants until everyone who worked on this goes to film school.
